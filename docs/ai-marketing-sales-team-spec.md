## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

QuanterraOS builds an AI-assisted go-to-market engine with the exact same discipline the core product is built upon:
* **Honest claims** derived from stored, reproducible data.
* **No fabricated partnerships** (e.g. no "hired Blackstone" claims).
* **No competitor comparisons** using other companies' names or logos.
* **No fake scarcity**, manufactured urgency, or countdown timers.
* **Code-enforced budget ceilings** applying the Rule B5 capital-lock pattern to advertising spend.

Rather than a generic, hallucinating "marketing AI," GTM is partitioned across **four narrow, tool-grounded agents**:


## 2. Competitive & Market Intelligence: What the Market Lacks

### A. The Prediction Market & Trading Signal Problem
* **The Competitor Playbook:** Generic signal channels, crypto bot marketplaces, and Telegram "alpha" rooms peddle unverified backtest screenshots, claim "95% win rates," and hide losing trades.
* **What the Market Lacks:** Traders and systematic liquidity providers are exhausted by fake alpha. They know 95%+ of bots lose to fees and spread. What they want is an **independent truth layer**: objective measurement of market calibration, live CME CF BRTI composite indices, and cross-venue spread tracking.
* **Our Moat:** We published our 1,316-market backtest showing our fair-value model lost to the Kalshi mid-price (0.2001 Brier vs 0.2500 baseline). **Transparency is our product.**

### B. The Enterprise AI Model Risk Problem (Lenders & Insurers)
* **The Competitor Playbook:** Big 4 accounting firms (PwC, EY, Deloitte, KPMG) charge $150k+ for annual static PDF model validation audits (SR 11-7).
* **The Regulatory Shift:**
  1. **NAIC Model Bulletin on AI:** 25 states adopted ongoing monitoring of underwriting, pricing, and claims models.
  2. **ECOA / Regulation B:** CFPB requires exact, verifiable adverse-action reason attribution. Model drift invalidates stated reasons.
  3. **Colorado SB 26-189:** 30-day plain-language notice requirements for automated adverse decisions.
* **What the Market Lacks:** Continuous, empirical calibration tracking between annual audits without exposing sensitive consumer PII.
* **Our Moat (TrustOS):** A 6-week, $20,000 fixed-fee pilot with a 50% milestone guarantee: if we fail to prove calibration on historical data, the second payment is waived. 100% credited toward annual monitoring.

### C. The B2B Outreach Problem
* **The Competitor Playbook:** Cold email spam generated by ungrounded LLMs claiming fake mutual connections, manufactured urgency ("only 2 slots left"), and fabricated case studies.
* **What Skeptical Buyers Respect:** Direct, brief communication citing real public data, verifiable regulatory citations, and an offer to review calibration math.

## 3. The Four Specialized Agents

### 1. Content & Copy Agent
* **Source Material:** Real telemetry from `findings.md`, `reports/btc15m-predictor-backtest-2026-10-03.txt`, `/predictions`, and `/calibration`.
* **Guardrails ([src/gtm-engine.ts](file:///c:/Users/enseg/OneDrive/Desktop/quanterraos-foundation/quanterraos-foundation/src/gtm-engine.ts)):**
  * Automatically scans for banned terms: `"beat the market"`, `"alpha"`, `"arbitrage"`, `"guaranteed"`, `"working your capital"`, `"citadel"`, `"blackstone"`, `"act now"`.
  * Flags manufactured urgency or countdown timers.
* **Human-in-the-Loop:** All drafts default to `PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`. No automated publishing to blogs or social channels.

### 2. Ad Platform Agent (Rule B5 Capital Lock Pattern)
* **Real Accounts:** Google Ads and Meta Ads verified accounts.
* **Code-Enforced Spend Caps:**
  * Default Daily Cap: **$100.00**
  * Default Monthly Cap: **$2,500.00**
  * Circuit lock active: Any requested spend exceeding daily or monthly ceilings is programmatically blocked (`BLOCKED_CAP_EXCEEDED`) and logged to `ad_spend_ledger`.
* **Destination Restriction:** Ads may only direct traffic to verified honest landing pages: `/pricing`, `/research`, `/calibration`, `/preview`, `/trustos`.

### 3. CRM & Outreach Agent
* **Lead Ingestion:** Automatically ingests signups from `/growth`, `/preview`, and `/trustos` into the `leads` table.
* **Automated Sequences:**
  * Welcome email on signup citing canonical figures (1,316 markets, 0.2001 Brier, $0.00 capital).
  * Day-7 telemetry check-in.
  * Pro Terminal usage nudge ($199/mo) with zero manufactured urgency.
* **Handoff:** Any incoming response or custom request immediately transitions to human review.

### 4. Sales Pipeline Agent ($750/mo Institutional & TrustOS Pilots)
* **Focus:** $750/month Institutional Tier and $20,000 TrustOS enterprise pilots.
* **Hard Rule:** `autoSendBlocked = 1`. Automated cold email blasts to quant desks or enterprise risk committees are strictly forbidden in code.
* **Research Dossier Generator:** Assembles public regulatory profiles, model risk exposure (SR 11-7, NAIC, ECOA), and agenda briefs for founder-led conversations.

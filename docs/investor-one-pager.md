# QuanterraOS
### Transparent Calibration Infrastructure for Short-Duration Prediction Markets
*As of October 4, 2026 · Confidential Discussion Document for Accredited Investors*

---

### What This Is
**QuanterraOS** is a specialized research and telemetry infrastructure built around 15-minute Bitcoin binary prediction markets on Kalshi (`KXBTC15M`). 

Our core value proposition is narrow, falsifiable, and auditable: **every prediction generated by the system is logged before the market settles, scored after settlement against CME CF BRTI official resolution, and never modified.** That immutable record—not an unverified marketing claim—is the foundation.

The platform currently operates three live, inspectable surfaces:
* **`/predictions`**: An append-only ledger of forecasts, timestamped pre-settlement, joined to the official CME CF BRTI settlement outcome once the market closes. Accessible live or as an auditable replay against a 1,316-market historical corpus, with the two strictly separated.
* **`/autopilot`**: An autonomous decision engine that runs on the live pipeline cycle, recording simulated paper orders and tracking cumulative hypothetical P&L against a passive market-baseline comparison. Hardcoded to **$0.00 real capital** under **Rule B5**—there is zero code path to route live exchange orders.
* **`/calibration`**: The audited Brier score breakdown (Murphy decomposition) across all settled markets, empirically testing where the market price is well-calibrated.

### Why This Is a Defensible Business
The investment thesis is **infrastructure and statistical transparency as the product**, rather than speculative trading returns:

1. **Category Moat**: There is currently no dedicated institutional calibration suite or auditable telemetry platform tailored for short-duration binary prediction markets.
2. **Falsifiable Integrity**: Pre-registered hypotheses, out-of-sample walk-forward testing, and an insert-only pre-settlement prediction ledger create an unassailable audit trail.
3. **Infrastructure for Future Edge**: If and when a strategy clears our pre-registered bar (positive Brier Skill Score and positive net expected value after fees on fresh data), the platform already possesses the full operational risk controls, data pipeline, and telemetry required to transition.

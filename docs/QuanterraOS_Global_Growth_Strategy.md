Build the **independent decision and risk companion people consult before entering a prediction-market position**. Start with Kalshi, then add eligible Polymarket audiences and other venues. The economic objective is a multibillion-dollar enterprise valuation; this document does not forecast that outcome or promise profitable trading. ## 3. Worldwide Market and Competitive Choice

Reported volume demonstrates attention, not software demand. Reuters reported Kalshi annualized volume of $178 billion and Polymarket April volume of $10.3 billion in May 2026. Annualized volume is a run-rate extrapolation; both figures include repeat turnover. Neither measures unique paying customers or QuanterraOS revenue opportunity ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/prediction-markets-see-surge-suspicious-trades-popularity-explodes-2026-05-15/)).

| Audience | Need | Entry Product | Expansion Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eligible U.S. Kalshi participants** | Understand friction and exposure | Free cost check and personal journal | Demonstrated activation and retention |
| **Eligible international Polymarket participants** | Unified cost, rule, and outcome view | Wallet/CSV analytics and localized explanations | Venue terms and country review |
| **Forecasting enthusiasts worldwide** | Learn without risking capital | Simulation and forecast journal | Local rules for education/data services |
| **Brokers and exchange frontends** | Embedded risk explanations | Licensed decision cards | Contracted data rights and integrations |
| **Professional teams** | Auditable research and outcomes | TrustOS/API pilots | Data quality, support, and procurement readiness |

Polymarket officially restricts order placement in numerous countries and regions. U.S. and international venue eligibility must be treated separately. Expand country by country, separating education, analytics, personalized recommendations, and execution ([Official geographic rules](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/geoblock)).

### Competition & Differentiation
Competition includes Unusual Whales prediction-market activity tools and Dome’s cross-platform data infrastructure. Basic odds aggregation, whale tracking, chat, and API access are insufficient differentiation. **QuanterraOS must own the user’s consented decision history, cost attribution, rule comprehension, and evidence of outcome improvement.**

## 4. The Product That Earns Signup

### Core Positioning
> **"Understand the real cost. Control your risk. Learn from every decision."**

Make the first useful result available before registration:
- User pastes a market link or enters a contract and sees: executable price, applicable fees, estimated slippage, maximum loss, breakeven probability, settlement source, and timestamp.
- Registration saves the check, starts a journal, and enables a weekly report.
- Never describe a user-entered probability as a validated model forecast.

### Build Order

| Order | Feature | User Benefit | Evidence Needed |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **True-Cost Check** | Reveals the hurdle to profitability | Reconcile against exchange fee schedules and fills |
| **2** | **Risk Plan** | Flags spending limits and correlated exposure | Track voluntary adherence and overrides |
| **3** | **Personal Outcome Journal** | Shows losses, fees, and recurring patterns | Reconcile imports with statements |
| **4** | **Settlement Explainer** | Makes contract wording understandable | Versioned rules and source links |
| **5** | **Realistic Paper Mode** | Practice without deposits | Latency, spreads, depth, fees, and missed fills |
| **6** | **Validated Forecast Comparison** | Tests whether forecasts add value | Prospective results after all costs |

- Use neutral labels: *"Costs checked,"* *"Uncertainty high,"* or *"No validated edge."* A green indicator must never imply safety or certainty.
- Budget alerts are advisory unless a venue integration actually enforces them.
- Binary contract expected profit arithmetic: For contract paying $1, expected profit = $p - \text{executable price} - \text{fees}$. At $0.51 purchase price and $0.0175 fee, breakeven is 52.75%; a genuine 55% win rate implies $0.0225 expected profit before other costs ([Official fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf)).

## 6. Distribution Strategy: Reach Users Where They Trade

1. **Indexable Content**: Publish cost calculators, settlement explainers, and fee comparisons answering specific search queries.
2. **Educator Pilot**: Partner with 10–20 small educators paid for qualified activated users or retained subscriptions (never trading volume).
3. **Shareable Educational Cards**: Clean summary cards without balances or sensational earnings claims.
4. **Prospective "Know Your Costs" Study**: 100–300 opt-in users tracking completion, attrition, fees, and outcomes.
5. **Embedded Cost/Risk Widgets**: Distribution cards for third-party frontends.
6. **Periodic Outcome Reports**: Public transparency on real market friction.

**Target Pilot Funnel (per 10,000 visits)**:
`10,000 visits` &rarr; `3,000 checks` &rarr; `900 registrations` &rarr; `450 journal activations` &rarr; `180 week-4 active` &rarr; `45 paid users`.

## 7. Pricing & Unit Economics

- **Free Tier**: Real-time cost visibility, essential risk education, and personal outcome tracking.
- **Plus Tier ($15/month)**: Enriched imports, automated weekly risk audits, and custom budget alerts.
- **Pro Tier ($39/month)**: Advanced microstructure analytics, cross-venue basis alerts, and deep historical exports.
- **Institutional ($750+/month)**: Custom data rights, API key access, and dedicated audit support.

### Unit Economics Model ($15/mo Plus)
- Monthly revenue: $15 &times; 80% gross margin = $12 gross profit.
- With 5% monthly churn &rarr; $240 gross-profit LTV.
- Target CAC &le; $60 yields 5-month gross-profit payback.
- At 5% free-to-paid conversion &rarr; $3.00 max spend per free signup.

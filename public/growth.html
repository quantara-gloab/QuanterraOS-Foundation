The team

Five agents, one accountable pipeline.

Each agent has a single job and a hard guardrail. Sentinel watches all of them and stops the line if anything doesn't add up.

01 · Scout

Prospect intake

Brings in the business contacts you're entitled to use — your CRM, inbound inquiries, licensed lists — and cleans, dedupes and screens them.

Personal inboxes and anyone who opted out are filtered before outreach.

02 · Herald

Outreach email

Writes short, specific notes for each prospect and follows up twice at most. A reply hands the thread to your team.

Honest subject lines, one-click unsubscribe, region rules and daily send caps.

03 · Concierge

Site chat

Answers visitor questions on your site around the clock and guides qualified visitors to sign up or request a call.

Speaks only from approved product facts. Never invents results or prices.

04 · Callback

Voice agent

Calls back people who asked for a call, answers questions in natural conversation and hands warm leads to a person.

Opt-in only. Says it's an AI up front, calls in local business hours, stops on request.

05 · Sentinel

Supervisor

Runs the schedule, tracks the funnel, retries what failed and reports what each agent did and why.

Verifies the consent ledger every cycle and halts outbound if it's been altered.

How it works

From list to booked conversation.

Load your audience

Import prospects from sources you can use. Scout screens them and marks which regions are eligible for outreach.

Reach out, carefully

Herald sends personal notes on a measured cadence. Every send records the rule that allowed it.

Convert interest

Concierge answers questions on-site. Visitors sign up and can ask for a call — consent captured word for word.

Talk and hand off

Callback rings them in business hours, then passes ready buyers to your team with the full transcript.

Compliance built in

Automation that stays on the right side of the line.

Most automated outreach gets blacklisted, fined or ignored. The Growth Engine is designed so your domain, numbers and reputation stay clean — and so you can prove it.

  • Tamper-evident consent ledgerEvery grant and opt-out is hash-chained with the exact wording shown, time and source.
  • Opt-in voice onlyThe AI never dials a cold number. It discloses that it's an AI and honors “stop calling” instantly.
  • One-click unsubscribeEvery email carries a working unsubscribe, your postal address and why the person is receiving it.
  • Region-aware rulesOutreach is enabled per country, so regions that require prior consent are held until you have it.
  • Fail-closed supervisorMissing settings or a broken ledger pause all outbound until fixed.

Regulatory Impetus

Why Now

Regulators now expect proof that automated AI decisions remain well-calibrated in production, not just validated once at launch:

  • Insurers (NAIC Model Bulletin): 25 states adopted as of July 2026. Mandates continuous monitoring of AI in underwriting, claims, and pricing, holding carriers accountable for vendor algorithms.
  • Lenders (ECOA & Regulation B): Adverse-action notices require specific, accurate reason attribution. When model scores drift, stated reasons become legally invalid.
  • Colorado SB 26-189: Plain-language notices required within 30 days of automated adverse decisions in lending and insurance (Effective Jan 1, 2027).
